\section*{Questions for Analysis}

\subsection*{Getting Started}

\textbf{Language}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Which terms in the text are unclear or unfamiliar? Collect them and look them up in a Bible dictionary.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Place \& Time}
\begin{enumerate}\setcounter{enumi}{1}
  \item Why is the place called ``the place of a skull'', and why was it outside the city (v.~22)?
  \item Collect the time references (vv.~25.~33.~34) -- how does the timeline run? Which festival is in the background?
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Context}
\begin{enumerate}\setcounter{enumi}{3}
  \item What happened immediately before (Mk 14:43--15:19)?
  \item ``Son of God'': Mk 1:1 $\leftrightarrow$ v.~39 -- who recognises it at the end, and why is that surprising?
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Characters}
\begin{enumerate}\setcounter{enumi}{5}
  \item Who acts, who speaks, who is silent in vv.~20--41? (When does Jesus himself speak?)
  \item Who is missing -- and why? (The disciples, Mk 14:50!)
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{The Crucifixion (vv.~20--28)}
\begin{enumerate}\setcounter{enumi}{7}
  \item Simon is \textbf{compelled} to carry the cross (v.~21) -- what does that mean for Mk 8:34 (``take up his cross'')?
  \item Why does Jesus refuse the wine mixed with myrrh (v.~23)?
  \item ``King of the Jews'' (v.~26) -- written as a charge: in what sense is it true?
  \item \emph{Our lives}: Simon carries the cross for Jesus -- unwillingly. What does that mean for our picture of discipleship: does it begin with us -- or is it demanded of us, and then grows?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Mocked on the Cross (vv.~29--32)}
\begin{enumerate}\setcounter{enumi}{11}
  \item Three groups mock -- what does each throw at him, where does something repeat itself?
  \item \textbf{The paradox of v.~31}: ``He saved others -- he cannot save himself.'' How can both be true at once? Is not-being-able-to-save-himself weakness -- or precisely the way he saves others?
  \item Where does the mockery become unintentionally true (vv.~29.~31.~32)?
  \item ``\ldots\ that we may see and believe'' (v.~32) -- why is that not a genuine condition of faith?
  \item \emph{Our lives}: Where do we (or others) demand proof first -- and what would it mean to believe without proof?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Darkness, Cry and Confession (vv.~33--41)}
\begin{enumerate}\setcounter{enumi}{16}
  \item ``Why have you forsaken me?'' (v.~34) -- mere despair? (Psalm 22: a lament that turns into praise.)
  \item Darkness and the torn veil (vv.~33.~38) -- who acts here, what is opened or judged?
  \item Why does the Roman centurion of all people confess ``Son of God'' (v.~39)?
  \item Why do the women stay and see everything (vv.~40f) -- unlike the disciples?
  \item \emph{Our lives}: When have we experienced God's nearness or forsakenness first-hand? What helped in the darkness?
  \item \emph{Our lives}: For whom can we today be there ``from a distance'' -- staying, watching, bearing witness?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Cross References to Other Bible Passages}
\begin{enumerate}\setcounter{enumi}{22}
  \item Read \textbf{Psalm 22} -- which verses of our text echo it? (Compare v.~29 with Ps 22:8; v.~24 with Ps 22:19; v.~34 with Ps 22:2.)
  \item \textbf{Isaiah 53}: Which features of the ``suffering servant of God'' (Isa 53:5.12) recur in our passage?
  \item \textbf{Mk 8:31; 9:31; 10:33f}: Jesus announces his suffering three times -- which of it is literally fulfilled in vv.~20--27?
  \item \textbf{Mk 10:45}: ``\ldots\ to give his life as a ransom for many'' -- how does this word sound in the light of vv.~37.39?
  \item \textbf{Heb 10:19f}: What does the torn veil (v.~38) mean in the light of this New Testament interpretation?
  \item \textbf{Amos 8:9 / Ex 10:21f}: Which ancient motif lies behind the darkness (v.~33)?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Closing}
\begin{enumerate}\setcounter{enumi}{28}
  \item The centurion recognises Jesus in the one who is dying. Where do we encounter people in ``crucified'' situations today?
\end{enumerate}
